In one sentence: At the end of the week, the assistant gathers work updates, groups them by project, and prepares a report draft for you to review before sharing.

💡 What it does

Weekly Report Assistant is an AI workflow built with Hermes Agent and a Lark bot. Once connected to the right sources, it can gather notes, project updates, and meeting takeaways, then organize them into an easy-to-read draft.

It helps with collecting and presenting information. You remain responsible for confirming progress, editing the content, and approving the final report.

⚙️ How it works

Collect

Gather relevant updates for the selected time period.

Check

Review the source, date, and status of each update.

Organize

Group information by project, result, plan, and blocker.

Draft

Follow a consistent format and include source references.

Review

Verify the facts, make edits, and decide whether to share.

📤 What the report includes

✅

🗓️

Next steps

⚠️

Blockers

Open issues and areas where support may be needed.

🔗

Sources

Links to notes, meetings, or project trackers.

🧭 Scope and guardrails

✓ What it can do

  • ✓Summarize information from connected sources.
  • ✓Group updates by project and suggest a structure.
  • ✓Flag missing details and items that need confirmation.
  • ✓Prepare a draft for the report owner to review.

! Human review matters

  • !It cannot invent progress when there is no source.
  • !It does not change the original project tracker.
  • !It does not share an unapproved report.
  • !It does not decide priorities on your behalf.

🚀 Getting started

In Lark, open a chat with the bot and ask it to prepare a report draft. You can also send short updates during the week for the assistant to include.
